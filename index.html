About Apex Detailing

Apex Detailing is a car detailing and ceramic coating workshop in Karachi. We clean, polish and protect cars so the paint looks showroom-new and stays that way.

What we offer

Seven services, from a quick wash to rust proofing and a 5-year ceramic coating.

Who we serve

Car owners in Karachi who want their paint restored and protected.

How we earn

We charge a fixed price per car. Our main costs are materials, rent and salaries, and what is left is our profit.
